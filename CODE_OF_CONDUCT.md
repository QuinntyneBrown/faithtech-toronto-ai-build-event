# Code of Conduct

Contributors of every background, belief, identity, ability, and experience level
are welcome. These expectations apply to issues, reviews, pull requests, and
other spaces where people represent this project.

## Participation

Be courteous, explain disagreements, give actionable feedback, and help newcomers.
Respect privacy, consent, and accessibility needs. Critique work with care and
accept corrections. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment,
disclosure of private information, repeated disruption, and retaliation are unacceptable.

## Reporting and response

Contact [Quinntyne Brown](mailto:quinntynebrown@gmail.com) privately with the subject
`FaithTech event platform conduct report`. The contact is also published in the
maintainer's [Studio conduct policy](https://github.com/QuinntyneBrown/quinntyne-brown-studio/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
Describe the incident and share only necessary evidence. If the concern involves
the maintainer, use [GitHub's reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

Maintainers should limit sharing to what investigation requires and apply these
standards to themselves. Responses may include a request to stop, content removal,
a warning, or temporary or permanent restrictions, depending on severity and recurrence.
Request reconsideration privately with relevant context; response timing depends on availability.
